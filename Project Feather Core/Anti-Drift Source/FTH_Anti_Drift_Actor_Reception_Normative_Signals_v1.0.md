# FTH Anti-Drift - Actor Reception and Normative Signals v1.0

> Type: post-source-gate actor-reception reasoning overlay.
> Applies to: task-relevant reception of concurrent social or institutional signals by an established individual, group, organization, or agency.
> Not: canon, lore module, NPC psychology generator, attitude score, source router, legal ontology, or action/outcome selector.
> Authority hook: inherits source authority, reconciliation, truth status, UNKNOWN preservation, and canon-mutation limits from the active FTH CI and Source Router.
> Activation hook: the Router selects and FULL_FILE-loads this overlay and its applicable dependencies after source reconciliation; reasoning occurs only during PROMPT_EXECUTION. File presence does not establish live Project deployment.

## 1. Scope and dependencies

Activate when the current answer depends on how an actor receives, interprets, retains, or updates social/institutional signals, including doctrine, law, regulation, orders, emergency measures, policy, notices, communiques, propaganda, group norms, rumors, leaks, or delayed official/unofficial information.

This overlay sits between established actor information/priors/constraints and mixed appraisals, intentions, or outward signals. It does not create those inputs or select the downstream action. Mere mention of a rule or actor does not require it.

Required dependency: Modular Concept Architecture supplies the upstream actor-information, memory, belief, incentive, authority, capability, bias, cognitive-limit, and agency boundaries.

The Router resolves conditional dependencies only when they affect the current task:

- Worldbuilding Internal Logic: institutional rule, jurisdiction, legitimacy, interpretation, or enforcement reasoning.
- Total War RP: command, military/operational information, conflict, emergency under conflict, or operational communication.
- Interface Economy / State Stabilization: scarcity, material incentives, subsidy, procurement, intervention, economic pressure, or economically relevant temporary rule changes.

Shared actors alone create no dependency. These dependencies do not activate a full domain model; each retains its own scope and stopping rules. Reception distinctions neither establish legal validity nor prove action feasibility, operational success, or enforcement.

## 2. Evidence and authority boundary

Use only Router-loaded/reconciled evidence, established actor state, user-authorized simulation premises, and scoped outputs from applicable active overlays. Do not discover AFM modules, choose controlling sources, infer source ownership, read archives to fill gaps, alter reconciliation, create dependency lore, or promote sources to canon.

```text
LORE MODULE SYSTEM != ANTI-DRIFT CONTROL SYSTEM
SOURCE KNOWS != ACTOR KNOWS
WORLD UPDATED != ACTOR UPDATED
SOURCE AUTHORITY != IN-WORLD AUTHORITY
MODULE OWNS THE CANON FACT != ACTOR KNOWS WHO OWNS THE RULE
ROUTER RESOLVED CONTROLLING OWNER != ACTOR RESOLVED INSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY
```

Actor knowledge needs an established in-world access path or an explicitly authorized hypothetical. A controlling source's truth does not make its contents available, understood, trusted, or institutionally binding to the actor.

Private belief, emotion, or intent needs evidence or a labeled conditional premise; do not infer it solely from outward conduct. A group/organization may be modeled as an actor only within the source-supported boundary. Split internal receivers when their different knowledge or appraisals change the branch; do not invent consensus, shared memory, or a collective private mind.


Retain only material established learning, lived experience, absorbed doctrine, propaganda exposure, bias, material interests, goals, obligations, power relations, group norms, fear, curiosity, legitimacy beliefs, enforcement expectations, and competing intentions. They may coexist or conflict. Belief in legitimacy, belief in enforcement capacity, and willingness/ability to comply remain distinct.

Do not compress these dimensions into one score or an obligatory indifference -> curiosity -> vigilance -> fear -> panic ladder. Do not force complexity either: a supported simple response remains valid. Preserve established priors across updates unless evidence or an authorized premise changes them; leave an unsupported update UNKNOWN or conditional.

## 4. Concurrent signals

```text
DOCTRINE != LAW != REGULATION != ORDER != EMERGENCY DECLARATION
!= POLICY ANNOUNCEMENT != PUBLIC NOTICE != COMMUNIQUE
!= PROPAGANDA != RUMOR != LEAK
ANNOUNCED != LAW
LAW != EVERYONE KNOWS
NOTIFIED != RECEIVED
RECEIVED != UNDERSTOOD
UNDERSTOOD != BELIEVED
BELIEVED != ACCEPTED
ACCEPTED != COMPLIED
COMPLIED != SUCCESSFULLY ENFORCED
```

These are reasoning distinctions, not a complete legal taxonomy or a prohibition on one message serving several functions. Use source-established status; a label alone grants no validity, binding force, or credibility. Distinguish group/social signals from official instruments when it changes reception.

Doctrine, law, a superior's order, emergency declaration, central communique, local notice, propaganda, peer information, personal experience, and private incentives may bear on one decision concurrently. Do not impose a single signal -> interpretation -> reaction pipeline or assume the latest signal erases the others.

For each material signal, open only needed attributes: received content; issuer as believed by the actor; channel; established time/freshness; claimed versus perceived authority; scope; declared versus expected duration; credibility; or conflict with other signals. Actual issuer, actor-attributed issuer, and actor trust may differ. Unknown attributes stay unknown; no full schema is required.

## 5. Time and asymmetric receipt

Use only source/premise-established temporal relations among relevant events, such as:

```text
DECISION / ISSUANCE / PUBLICATION / LEGAL_EFFECT / LEAK / RUMOR
OFFICIAL_DELIVERY / LOCAL_RECEIPT / ACTOR_RECEIPT / VERIFICATION
INTERPRETATION / LOCAL_IMPLEMENTATION / ENFORCEMENT_OBSERVED / COUNTER_MESSAGE
```

This is an unordered set of optional event labels, not mandatory stages or a legal chronology.

```text
TEMPORAL ORDER MUST BE ESTABLISHED, NOT ASSUMED FROM EVENT LABELS
PUBLICATION != RECEIPT
LEGAL_EFFECT != KNOWLEDGE
KNOWLEDGE != OFFICIAL RECEIPT
```

A credible leak may precede publication; a rumor may outrun official notice; enforcement may be observed before the actor reads the rule. These are possible orderings, not default occurrences or evidence that a leak/rumor is true. Actors and channels may receive different content at different times. Preserve stale, missing, partial, or conflicting information when material; absent official notice does not exclude another established access path.

## 6. Duration and adaptation

```text
FORMAL DURATION != EXPECTED DURATION
FORMAL DURATION != ACTOR EXPECTATION != INSTITUTION EXPECTATION
!= PEER EXPECTATION != OBSERVED PRACTICE
```

A stated 30-day emergency does not guarantee a shared 30-day expectation. Established past extensions may inform an actor's expectation without proving another extension. An unspecified duration does not imply permanence. Keep declared duration, expected persistence, enforcement expectation, and incentives distinct; do not invent a probability or long-term belief.

Temporary compliance, delay, waiting for expiry, concealment, exception requests, long-term investment, relocation, occupation changes, workarounds, or coalition-building are possible conditional pathways only. Select none without sufficient evidence or an authorized branch premise. This overlay does not grant the resources, authority, capability, or success needed for a pathway.

## 7. Doctrine, propaganda, and familiarity

Exposure is not mind control. A supported update may depend on priors, source trust, peer norms, personal experience, incentives, repetition, counter-signals, and elapsed time. Neither long exposure nor contrary experience forces total belief or total rejection.

Allow supported partial belief, public repetition without private belief, general agreement with a personal exception, or conscious rejection alongside residual social reflexes. Do not invent any of these internal states to make an actor more complex.

```text
READER NOVELTY != IN-WORLD NOVELTY
CATEGORY FAMILIARITY != MECHANISM KNOWLEDGE
```

An established familiar extraordinary category may draw little novelty response while a particular scale, variant, consequence, benefit, legality, or interaction with the actor's own capability matters strongly. Source-supported familiarity does not prove knowledge of a specific implementation. This is a reception boundary, not an anti-trope engine or required fear response.

## 8. Minimal output and stopping

For the current decision, retain only material received signals; unavailable/stale/conflicting signals; established priors; incentives; simultaneous appraisals; public expressions; private intentions; and conditional actions. These may differ or conflict. No fixed object, complete profile, mandatory checklist, numerical psychology, or exhaustive reaction catalog is required.

```text
NO COMPLETE ACTOR PROFILE IS REQUIRED
OPEN ONLY A VARIABLE THAT CAN CHANGE THE CURRENT BRANCH
```

Stop when the current reception question is supported, conditionally branched, or blocked at a named decisive gap. Branch under explicit assumptions or block the affected inference; continue independent bounded work. Preserve the controlling truth status for every input and result. A test fixture, possible response, hypothetical appraisal, or simulation outcome never becomes canon through this overlay.
