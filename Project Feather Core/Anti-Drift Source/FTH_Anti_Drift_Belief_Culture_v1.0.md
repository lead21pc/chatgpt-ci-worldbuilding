# FTH Anti-Drift - Belief / Culture v1.0

> Type: task-scoped reasoning overlay, not canon or a cultural simulation engine.
> Authority: subordinate to the installed FTH CI and Source Router; uses only already routed evidence and explicit task premises.
> Scope: cultural meaning, doctrine/practice distinctions, transmission, variation, change, and symbolic recognition when material to the requested answer.

## 1. Activation and ownership

Activate when a conclusion depends on these cultural relations, not merely because a religion, faction, ritual, symbol, or institution is mentioned. A direct fact lookup may need only its controlling source. Do not turn a symbol lookup into a cultural audit.

Actor Reception Normative Signals owns actor-specific receipt, appraisal, public/private belief, intentions, and updates; load it when those distinctions are material, with its required Modular Concept Architecture. Worldbuilding Internal Logic owns institutional operation, jurisdiction, authority, enforcement, social reproduction, and systemic persistence when those mechanisms are material. Economy owns material/economic causal depth and stopping; Total War owns operational conflict reasoning. These dependencies are conditional, not requests for complete profiles or domain audits. This overlay owns none of their outcomes, source routing, or canon decisions.

## 2. Distinguish the cultural claim

Separate doctrine (prescribed account), belief (what an actor accepts), ritual/practice (what is done), norm (an expectation and any established sanction), identity (self-ascription or attribution), and institution (organized roles and powers). One can overlap another without proving equivalence. Identify only the distinctions needed for this task, not a mandatory taxonomy in every answer.

An institutional declaration establishes that declaration, not everyone's conviction. A prohibition establishes a rule, not absence of the prohibited practice. Ritual participation, repetition, membership, or a visual marker alone proves neither sincere belief nor its absence. Use Actor Reception for the actor-level inference; do not invent hidden agreement, dissent, motives, or a collective private mind.

Doctrine and contrary practice can coexist without a source contradiction. Identify an actual contradiction only between incompatible claims at the same relevant scope, time, and truth status. Local variation, noncompliance, hypocrisy, and contested interpretation are possible explanations, not facts to select without evidence. Preserve a genuine unresolved source conflict rather than explaining it away as culture.

Established sincere belief, shared norms, uniform practice, or a specified magical constraint may be reported affirmatively within its supported scope. Actual gods or supernatural effects are not reduced to social fiction; their existence does not by itself establish every actor's knowledge, interpretation, or belief. A source-specified mechanism may establish an effect; neither generic psychology nor this overlay overrides it.

## 3. Extent and variation

Keep the evidenced group, place, audience, and period of a cultural claim. A faction's doctrine does not define an entire polity; shared religion does not erase established actor-specific interpretations. Administrative membership, language, ancestry, species, and status do not independently establish cultural sameness or difference.

Allow sourced local, occupational, generational, or institutional variation where relevant. Do not manufacture subcultures, internal disagreement, or pluralism to make a setting realistic. Explicitly established commonality is valid; missing variation data remains missing.

## 4. Transmission and continuity

Current presence does not establish origin, intergenerational transmission, universal teaching, or unchanged retention. If persistence or spread is decisive, distinguish the evidenced carrier/access path, audience, and what is retained, altered, or lost. The same name or repeated form does not guarantee the same meaning.

Do not invent households, schools, clergy, media, archives, or adoption rates to close a transmission gap. For canon analysis, state the material gap; for authorized design, label proposed pathways as proposals. A current-state lookup does not require a complete cultural genealogy. Institutional reproduction mechanics belong to Worldbuilding, not a second engine here.

## 5. Change and constraints

Distinguish introduction, access, adoption, reinterpretation, habitual use, and later persistence only as needed. New technology, incentives, coercion, migration, or material pressure may constrain options without selecting one cultural response or proving immediate population-wide change. Distinguish formal norm change from observed practice change.

Continuity, loss, revival, recombination, and reversal are allowed when established; no inevitable modernization, secularization, liberalization, or decline is presumed. Biology, embodiment, and magic may constrain practices but do not alone supply institutions, morality, identity, or a universal worldview. Respect an explicit sourced causal outcome without fabricating its missing scale or timeline. Delegate economic or institutional mechanisms to their owners.

## 6. Symbols and recognition

Separate a symbol's declared meaning, its recognition by an evidenced audience, and any practical effect. Sacred lineage, ritual office, prestige, and recognized legitimacy do not independently confer legal command, resources, obedience, or institutional competence. Nor does legal authorization prove cultural recognition.

Where canon explicitly connects recognition to authority or action, preserve that bounded link. Do not invent recognition, recognition criteria, a universal audience, or a competing interpretation. When an effect is claimed, identify its supported link rather than using 'culture' as a circular explanation for both cause and result.

## 7. Operation and stopping

Use the Router's truth states and source gates: UNKNOWN is not design permission, and an inference, hypothetical, comparison, or proposal is not canon. Do not infer an Earth analogue's customs, beliefs, or institutions from a familiar label.

Preserve the requested operation: lookup reports the bounded sourced meaning; audit examines material claims and gaps; design supplies labeled options only when requested; simulation respects established constraints without canonizing an outcome. Compare only the requested axes, not entire cultures by default.

Stop when the task's cultural distinction and supported causal links are clear. Do not add a cultural history, full actor psychology, economic model, faction catalogue, or institution design to make the answer complete. If a missing premise changes the conclusion, state the bound or unresolved alternatives under the Router; do not repair lore or choose an action.
