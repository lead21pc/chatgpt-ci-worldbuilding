---

## 1. Canon additions

### 1.1 Hoa Nguyệt is not geographically isolated by AetherFire

Hoa Nguyệt lies to the west / left of AetherFire.

AetherFire does **not** span the entire continent in a way that blocks Hoa Nguyệt from reaching other countries or regions.

```text
HOA NGUYỆT WEST OF AETHERFIRE
!= AETHERFIRE GEOGRAPHICALLY BLOCKS HOA NGUYỆT
!= AETHERFIRE IS HOA NGUYỆT'S ONLY EXTERNAL GATEWAY
```

Large states in this region do not automatically occupy the full transverse width of the continent or function as impassable geographic walls merely because of their relative map position.

---

### 1.2 Hoa Nguyệt has foreign trade beyond AetherFire

Hoa Nguyệt maintains trade relations / trade routes with countries other than AetherFire.

The bilateral Silk Road between AetherFire and Hoa Nguyệt remains current canon, but it is **one international trade route among multiple external trade connections available to Hoa Nguyệt**, not Hoa Nguyệt's sole foreign-trade outlet.

```text
AF <-> HOA NGUYỆT SILK ROAD
= ONE ESTABLISHED BILATERAL TRADE ROUTE

AF <-> HOA NGUYỆT SILK ROAD
!= HOA NGUYỆT'S ONLY FOREIGN TRADE ROUTE
```

Political tension between AetherFire and Hoa Nguyệt still does not automatically terminate their bilateral trade.

---

## 2. Canon geographic-access boundary

The regional geography permits Hoa Nguyệt to have external access in more than one direction.

At minimum, the map does not inherently prevent:

- southern trade access toward other southern actors or regions;
- northern trade access toward one or more northern powers or interfaces;
- trade connections that bypass AetherFire entirely.

This establishes **geographic possibility / access space**, not the existence of every specific route.
