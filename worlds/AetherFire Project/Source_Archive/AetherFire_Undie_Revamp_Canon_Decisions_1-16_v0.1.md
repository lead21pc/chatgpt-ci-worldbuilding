# 1. Legal ontology of Undie

## CURRENT CANON

`Undie` is a **professional ecosystem / occupational identity**.

It is not:

- a legal status;
- a Slave class;
- a social caste;
- a punishment status.

A person can simultaneously be:

```text
Citizen
+
Undie professional
```

The state may regulate:

- professional activity;
- contracts;
- venues;
- operators;
- licensing;
- certification;
- safety;
- taxation;
- restricted services.

But:

```text
PROFESSIONAL AFFILIATION
!= LEGAL IDENTITY OF THE PERSON
```

The prior ontology:

```text
Undie
IS_A
Slave class
```

is superseded.

# 2. Legacy rank / color hierarchy

## CURRENT CANON

The old system-wide hierarchy:

```text
Red
→ Pink
→ Gray
→ Purple
→ Hazel
→ White
```

is retired as a universal Undie career ladder.

There is no longer a required system-wide progression ending in White.

`White → Citizen` is retired.

If old colors are reused in the future, they may only return through a separate explicit canon decision, for example as:

- branding;
- certification;
- local organizational code;
- context-specific signaling.

They do not automatically restore:

- legal hierarchy;
- social hierarchy;
- universal career progression;
- exit-to-Citizen mechanics.

# 3. Professional branches

## CURRENT CANON

Undie is a **multi-track, overlapping professional ecosystem**.

A professional may participate in multiple types of work.

Sex work is only one possible branch.

```text
SEX WORK
IS_A possible Undie branch

SEX WORK
!= Undie as a whole
```

The system is not defined around one mandatory career ladder.

Examples previously discussed include:

- performance;
- dance;
- singing;
- hosting;
- companionship;
- artistic or cultural entertainment;
- private entertainment;
- intimate services;
- other gray-zone professional activity.

These examples establish breadth, not a closed exhaustive taxonomy.

No final branch taxonomy is canonized by this file.

---

# 4. Entry and exit

## CURRENT CANON

Entering Undie means entering a profession or professional ecosystem.

Possible interfaces may include, where later specified:

```text
training
contract
registration
professional affiliation
licensing
certification
```

Entry does not change citizenship or legal status.

Leaving Undie may mean:

```text
leave
retire
go inactive
change profession
change affiliation
```

A professional has legal freedom to leave the profession unless another independently valid legal constraint applies.

However:

```text
LEGAL FREEDOM TO LEAVE
!= PRACTICAL COST-FREE EXIT
```

Practical exit may be affected by:

- contract obligations;
- debt;
- advance repayment;
- reputation;
- patronage;
- network dependency;
- other traceable professional or financial consequences.

## RETIRED AS STATUS TRANSITIONS

```text
Citizen → Undie
Civil → Undie
Yellow → Red / Undie
White / Undie → Citizen
```

These old relations no longer operate as legal-status transitions.

# 5. Criminal interface

## CURRENT CANON

The old ontology rule:

```text
Criminal cannot transfer to Undie
```

is retired.

The correct question is now whether a person with a criminal record is eligible for a particular professional activity, license, venue, contract, or access level.

Where later established, restrictions may depend on:

- type of conviction;
- safety risk;
- access risk;
- profession;
- venue;
- licensing rules.

Criminal record does not create a special status-transition graph with Undie.

---

# 6. Reproduction and sterilization

## CURRENT CANON

Mandatory irreversible sterilization of Undie professionals is retired and superseded.

Fertility is not determined by Undie professional membership.

```text
UNDIE MEMBERSHIP
!= FERTILITY STATUS
```

Contraception, pregnancy, reproductive healthcare, fertility treatment, or reproductive restrictions must belong to their own relevant scope, such as:

- medical;
- personal;
- contract-specific;
- branch-specific;
- other explicitly established law.

No old compulsory reproductive regime is restored automatically.

---

# 7. Credit, debt, obligation, and favor

## CURRENT CANON

The old Undie-specific progression/economic web is retired.

The monetary baseline is:

```text
CASH
= basic money

CREDITS
= state-backed electronic money
```

The following old Undie-specific architecture is retired as current baseline:

- `Credit Score`;
- `Contribution Points`;
- status-collateral `Credit Line`;
- progression through an Undie-specific credit web.

The system must distinguish:

```text
FINANCIAL DEBT
!= CONTRACTUAL OBLIGATION
!= SOCIAL FAVOR
!= POLITICAL FAVOR
```

Favor is not a currency and has no default unit.

Debt may produce a traceable consequence such as:

- repayment;
- service;
- concession;
- access obligation;
- renegotiated trade-off.

But:

```text
DEBT
!= OWNERSHIP
```

and:

```text
DEBT
!= AUTOMATIC SEXUAL SERVICE
```

No human/status collateral is part of the current Undie baseline.

# 8. Gray market / black market

## CURRENT CANON

The old black-market interpretation:

```text
Citizen demand
→ fake Slave / fake Undie experience
```

is retired.

The new primitive is an **off-book / gray access economy**.

Depending on later canon, such markets may involve:

- anonymity;
- exclusivity;
- private access;
- off-contract service;
- unlicensed service;
- restricted experience;
- discreet introductions;
- private or restricted venues.

These are categories of possible market function, not a finalized commodity list.

Citizen-level demand for gray/black-market activity remains structurally relevant.

# 9. Professional organizations

## CURRENT CANON

Undie professionals may operate through professional intermediaries or independently.

Possible forms may include:

- agency;
- salon;
- House;
- cooperative;
- studio;
- other intermediary;
- independent operator.

No single organizational form is mandatory for all Undie professionals.

`House` is not established here as the universal official name.

The old ownership/master relation is retired.

```text
PROFESSIONAL INTERMEDIARY
!= OWNER OF PERSON
```

# 10. Undi

## CURRENT CANON

Undi is no longer a Slave uniform or legal-status marker.

Where used, it functions as a **professional uniform family / professional visual interface**.

Its default design direction is contextual or duty-based use rather than permanent identity marking.

```text
DUTY / CONTEXT USE
!= MANDATORY 24/7 IDENTITY MARKING
```

The following old functions are retired as default Undie architecture:

- compulsory collar solely for Undie identification;
- compulsory identification ink;
- compulsory body marking;
- low-coverage visual humiliation;
- public-readability humiliation apparatus.

## Hoa Nguyệt relation

AetherFire intentionally borrowed / appropriated design ideas from Hoa Nguyệt national dress when developing Undi.

However:

```text
ACTUAL AF INTENT
!= EXTERNALLY PROVABLE INTENT
```

Therefore Undi can create:

- cultural grievance;
- diplomatic friction;
- accusation of appropriation;
- political dispute;
- plausible deniability.

It is not an openly self-proving humiliation declaration.

# 11. Security architecture

## CURRENT CANON

AetherFire security response is not a unitary actor.

```text
AF SECURITY RESPONSE
!= UNITARY ACTOR
```

Different institutions may possess different:

- information;
- jurisdiction;
- authority;
- command;
- objectives;
- risk tolerance;
- political incentives.

Coordination may:

- succeed;
- fail;
- overlap;
- interfere;
- escalate;
- produce friendly-fire-like institutional failures.

Do not infer:

```text
ONE AGENCY KNOWS
→ WHOLE STATE KNOWS
```

or:

```text
ONE STATE INTEREST
→ ALL AGENCIES EXECUTE ONE POLICY
```

A specific friendly-fire or code-blue incident is not created by this decision alone.

---

# 12. Death handling

## CURRENT CANON

Undie no longer has a special disposal regime.

Death involving an Undie professional must route through the ordinary applicable death / legal / investigative framework plus any genuinely relevant professional interfaces, such as:

- venue responsibility;
- employer/operator responsibility;
- medical investigation;
- evidence custody;
- family/property interface;
- security or counterintelligence involvement when a valid trigger exists.

The old default:

```text
death
→ paperwork
→ disappear
```

is retired.

Security involvement requires a causal reason; it is not automatic because the deceased or incident involved Undie.

# 13. Opposition

## CURRENT CANON

There is no single mandatory universal `Anti-Undie Institution`.

Opposition to, criticism of, regulation of, defense of, or reform pressure around Undie may arise from multiple independent actors.

Possible motives may include:

- religious;
- labor;
- civic;
- cultural;
- nationalist;
- security;
- professional;
- economic.

No old Temple, Purple committee, or other legacy body is automatically restored by this decision.

---

# 14. Resistance interface

## CURRENT CANON

Undie does not inherently generate resistance.

The old causal default:

```text
humiliation
→ resistance
```

is retired.

However, an Undie professional network may provide interfaces such as:

- mobility;
- social access;
- private venues;
- money flow;
- gray-market contacts;
- interpersonal networks.

These interfaces may be used or exploited by:

- resistance;
- security;
- political actors;
- foreign actors;

when a valid access path exists.

```text
UNDIE NETWORK
CAN INTERFACE WITH RESISTANCE

UNDIE NETWORK
!= RESISTANCE BY DEFAULT
```

# 15. Scandal, statecraft, intelligence, and access

## CURRENT CANON

Undie has no default profession called `Spy Undie`.

Political and intelligence consequences emerge from access and relationships rather than defining the profession itself.

Possible pathway:

```text
profession
+ access
+ money
+ private environment
+ relationships
+ reputation
→ possible political consequence
```

Possible consequences may include, where causally supported:

- scandal;
- influence;
- lobbying;
- leak;
- compromising situation;
- political setup;
- intelligence interest;
- diplomatic consequence.

But:

```text
ACCESS
!= KNOWLEDGE
!= INTELLIGENCE ROLE
!= AUTHORITY
```

Undie can reach diplomacy/statecraft through emergent access politics, not through a universal intelligence function.

# 16. MC2 boundary

## CURRENT CANON

MC2 is no longer routed through Undie as punishment, status degradation, or mandatory sex work.

She does not need to lose legal status in order to enter or interface with the Undie ecosystem.

If MC2's future pathway passes through Undie, it must operate as a professional / social / gray-zone access pathway.

Current direction:

```text
MC2
→ needs access toward the maternal / Raging Fire / AF political pathway
→ formal routes may be unavailable, too visible, or too politically dangerous
→ Undie ecosystem can provide a gray professional/social access environment
→ political pathway can develop through that interface
```

This file does not yet establish:

- MC2's exact profession;
- cover;
- organization;
- operator;
- patron;
- first contact;
- exact gray-market activity;
- exact resistance contact;
- exact security incident;
- exact path to her mother;
- final outcome.

Those belong to the dedicated MC2 pathway design.
